Food Crisis in Turkey

Turkey is facing devastating food shortages with food loss rates of up to 70%. These shortages are not only leading to 2.5% of the country in malnourishment but also leading to 5 Billion USD in economic losses and intensifying inflation.

Our mission is to help the people of turkey find access to affordable food local to them. Our solution is to evaluate an individuals budget and location to be able to inform them of the closest and cheapest place to get clean water near them.
