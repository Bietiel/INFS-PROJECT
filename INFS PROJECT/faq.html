Learn more about the food and water challenges in Turkey and how to use our website.

Water Crisis

Turkey has been experiencing water shortages, with rainfall 27% below the average. The country is also at risk of a severe drought by 2030, creating concerns for its lakes, farmers, and communities.
Our website is designed to consider a person's budget and location to help identify the closest and cheapest places to access clean water.

Food Crisis

Inflation has increased food costs, making it harder for people to afford everyday groceries. More than 25% of consumers in Turkey report that cost is a factor in their purchasing decisions.
Turkey produces several agricultural products, including wheat, sugar beets, milk, poultry, cotton, tomatoes, and fruits. However, food shortages and food loss continue to create challenges.

Using Our Website

Visit the Food or Water page to learn about the challenges in Turkey. You can also enter your name, email, budget, and location in the form to provide information for the project's assistance concept.
Your budget helps identify affordable options, while your location helps determine which food or water resources may be closest to you.
