Food Crisis

Turkey is a contry that heavily produces wheat,sugar,beets,milk,poultry,cotton,tomatoes and fruits. Turkey relies on farm grown produce to feed it's citizens and recently inflation has increased by 27.73 percent in Octuber of 2026 over the same month in the previous year. Highest it had been reported was in November of 2022 which led to a report of an inflantion rate of 102.55 and is expected to be at a 34.00 percent by the end of this quarter. As budget constraints began to rise more citizens are making selective choices towards what they purchse. Turkish food industry reveal that over 25% of consumers reports that cost is an factor in their purchse. Making that any low income Turkey citizen may suffer for the increase of inflation as they suffer to find affordable food options.

Water Shortage

Turkey has been suffering from water shortage for the past few years and making rainfall 27% below the long-term average. In Turkey, it has suffered as 36 lakes have been completely dried up over the past 50 years and are in risk of 14 more lakes began to dry up. Farmers are suffering as its predicted that for farmers to have an successful produce outcome they'll need an jump from 25% to 60% for them to produce successfully.
