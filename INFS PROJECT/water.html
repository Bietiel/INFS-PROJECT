Turkey Water Crisis

Turkey is facing water scarcity and is at risk of a severe drought by 2030. 186 natural lakes out of 240 have dried up in the past 60 years. This is very concerning for the people of Turkey. Our mission is to help the people of Turkey to find access to clean and affordable water. Our solution is to evaluate an individuals budget and location to be able to inform them of the closest and cheapest place to get clean water near them.
