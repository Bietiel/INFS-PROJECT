About Us

Learn more about Food & Water Turkey

Our website provides information and helpful sources to help people in Turkye find affordable food options and clean drinking-water resources. Visitors can explore available resources, learn where to find them, and make choices based on their location and budget.

Meet Our Team

We are five Middle Tennessee State University students working together to develop this website to make useful food and water information easier to find.
